2. BREAK-THEN-CONFIRM — does this driver actually see a difference?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A byte-identical dump proves nothing until the comparison is shown to fail on a
deliberate mutation. Mutation applied to components/Receipts.tsx: the single
JSX space separator in the receipts tag widened by one character,
  ▸ {citations.length}{" "}   ->   ▸ {citations.length}{"  "}
i.e. exactly the class of whitespace slip a "verbatim lift" could introduce and
the smallest one that could.

$ cmp -s markup-before.html markup-mutant.html && echo IDENTICAL || echo DIFFER
DIFFER - driver sees it

$ diff markup-before.html markup-mutant.html | grep -c '^[<>]'
14752

Sample of the change, from appid 10 (Counter-Strike):
  before:  <span class="tag mono">▸ 2 reviews</span>
  mutant:  <span class="tag mono">▸ 2  reviews</span>

$ # mutation reverted, re-rendered
$ cmp -s markup-before.html markup-revert.html && echo IDENTICAL
IDENTICAL again

RESULT: the driver fails loudly on a one-character change (14,752 changed
lines) and returns to identical on revert. The section-1 result is therefore
evidence and not an artefact of a comparison that cannot fail.
