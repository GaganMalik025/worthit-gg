ΔE is CIE76 in Lab. 15.6 and 13.1 against --surface put the tint far past the ~2.3 just-noticeable difference, so it reads as a tinted panel rather than a guess; ΔE 16.0 between the two washes means the boxes are told apart by tint before the glyph is consulted. 85 and 70 against the stamp colours are the numbers that matter for the constraint: what is on screen is nowhere near the colour it might be confused with, and even the rails — the strongest expression of the tint — sit 63 and 48 away. Text contrast on the washes: --text 12.0:1 and 12.8:1, --text-dim 4.7:1 and 5.0:1 — AA clear, the dim heading included.

The hue stays in the green/red family on purpose — that is what makes the polarity legible without a caption. The separation from the stamp is carried by the other two axes: saturation (69%/91% on the stamp against 20% here) and form — the stamp colours a bold 24px word inside a bordered chip on a transparent background; the boxes colour only a background wash and a rail, and never touch a character of text. Panel 3 is the check that none of this leans on hue alone: under deuteranopia the two washes stay ΔE 11.1 apart and the rails 29.9, and the ▲ / ▼ glyphs carry the rest.
